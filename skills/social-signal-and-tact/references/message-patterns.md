# Message Patterns

## 1. Specific credit → impact → next step

Use for teachers/leaders after useful guidance.

Structure:

`您刚才提到的 X 我回去重新看了一下，确实把 Y 这个问题想清楚了。接下来我先按这个方向把 Z 做出来，完成后再跟您汇报。`

Why it works: praise is embedded in a real effect.

## 2. Soft sandwich

Use when a request or correction could feel abrupt.

`认可/感谢 → 具体请求 → 信任/积极收尾`

Avoid making the praise unrelated to the request.

## 3. Ask-for-advice frame

Use when the other person has expertise or authority.

`我现在在 A 和 B 之间有点拿不准。结合您之前提到的 X，我更偏 A，但担心 Y。您觉得我这样判断有没有明显问题？`

This gives respect without pretending helplessness.

## 4. Update with credit

`上次您提醒我注意 X，我这两天按这个思路改了 Y，现在结果是 Z。这个点确实帮我少走了不少弯路。`

Useful for maintaining relationships without empty “老师好久不见”。

## 5. Face-saving disagreement

`我大体理解您的思路。我现在有一个实际情况是 X，所以我想先试 Y，看看能不能把 Z 验证出来。`

State the constraint and experiment. Do not frame the other person as wrong.

## 6. Warm familiarity without faking history

Use the person's actual known style or a real recent interaction:

`您上次那个判断我这两天越做越觉得准。`

Do not invent “还记得我们以前……” unless that event is real and supplied.

## 7. Micro-praise

For strong relationships, one short phrase is enough:

- `这个判断确实很准。`
- `这个角度我之前真没想到。`
- `您这个提醒很及时。`
- `这件事您看得比我远。`

The compliment works because it names the value.

## 8. Gift delivery → relational follow-up

Use when sending a modest gift, local specialty, fruit, health product, or courtesy item to a teacher/leader/senior person.

First send the immediate fact and enough product context:

`老师好，您现在不在办公室，我就先把东西放在您办公室了。前几天家里托 X 那边的朋友买了些当地的 Y，这两天刚好熟了/到了，味道很好，我给您也带了一些。旁边还有 Z，也是前段时间专门给您准备的，想着您平时工作比较忙，也希望您多注意身体。`

Then, if there is additional relationship context, send it separately after a reply or as a second message:

`他最近一直在外地忙 X，这段时间没能抽出时间过来看您，也一直觉得有些过意不去，让我代他向您问好。您回办公室后可以尝尝呀，希望您吃着喜欢。`

Why it works: logistics, gift explanation, apology and relational warmth are distributed across chat turns, so the message does not read like a ceremonial speech.

Key edits learned from a real case:

- Remove directive endings such as `记得拿一下`; use invitational wording such as `可以尝尝呀，希望您吃着喜欢`.
- Remove unnecessary prestige signaling such as price, “international brand”, rankings, or “top-tier supplement”.
- Remove specific medical-effect claims from gift messages unless the user needs a factual health explanation in another context.
- Do not overstate apology for a missed courtesy visit. `有些过意不去` + `代他向您问好` is often enough.
- Keep accurate provenance. If a gift came through a friend in a place, say `托那边的朋友买的当地特产`; do not imply the giver personally lives or traveled there.
- Specific timing can improve sincerity when it matters, e.g. `最近一直在外地准备毕业答辩` or an upcoming date, but include only details that the recipient needs.

## 9. Holiday gratitude with one small memory

Use for Teachers' Day, graduation, year-end thanks, or another occasion where the goal is sincere gratitude rather than a request.

Structure:

`节日问候 → 一件很小但真实的记忆 → 这件事对自己的影响 → 简短感谢/祝福`

Example:

`柴老师，教师节快乐！之前有一次活动结束后坐您的车回来，路上您跟我说过一句“以后不能只做一个码农”，还提醒我要多培养真正解决问题的能力。那句话我后来一直记着，在我考虑实习和就业方向的时候也经常会想起来。很感谢您一直以来给我的关心和指导，也祝您教师节快乐，工作顺利，身体健康！`

Why it works: one concrete memory makes the gratitude believable. The memory should be short; the emotional center remains `我记得、它影响了我、谢谢您`.

Do not decorate the ending with formulaic parallelism. If `我也会继续努力把事情做好` already closes the message, stop there. Avoid generated-sounding endings such as `把事情做好，把路走稳`, `做好当下，走好未来`, or repeated `把A，把B` structures that add symmetry without adding meaning.

## Anti-patterns

Avoid:

- “您高瞻远瞩、英明指导、让我受益匪浅” without specific evidence;
- three consecutive sentences of praise before the actual request;
- fake vulnerability designed only to manipulate;
- copying corporate email language into WeChat;
- calling someone a close friend when the relationship is not close;
- `您回来记得拿一下` in a gift message to a senior person;
- explaining how expensive, premium, rare, or medically powerful a gift is;
- putting delivery, apology, family background, product benefits, and closing wishes into one overloaded WeChat paragraph;
- slogan-like paired endings such as `把A做好，把B走稳`, especially when the second half exists only to make the sentence sound complete.
