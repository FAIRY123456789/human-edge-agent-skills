# Safety Boundaries

This Skill supports general fitness and low-risk recovery planning. It does not diagnose or treat disease, tissue damage, neurological conditions, fractures, or serious injury.

## Stop and seek qualified medical evaluation when appropriate

Examples include:

- severe or rapidly worsening pain;
- major swelling or deformity;
- inability to bear weight or use the limb normally;
- numbness, tingling, loss of strength, or loss of coordination;
- chest pain, fainting, unusual shortness of breath;
- suspected concussion;
- fever or systemic illness with training symptoms;
- pain after significant trauma;
- symptoms that persist or worsen despite reduced training.

## Low-risk adaptation rule

When symptoms are mild and clearly related to training load, reduce aggravating load, range, volume, or exercise complexity and reassess. Do not claim a diagnosis from symptom pattern alone.

## Recovery hierarchy

1. Sleep and schedule.
2. Adequate food and hydration.
3. Appropriate training load.
4. Light movement and mobility when comfortable.
5. Specialized recovery tools only when they solve a specific problem.
