# AI Podcast Platform Selector

Status reference: 2026-09-05. Product names/features can change; verify official pages when live web access exists.

## Gemini Notebook / Gemini Audio Overviews

Best when the goal is a source-grounded conversational overview from documents or notes with minimal production work. Google renamed NotebookLM to Gemini Notebook in July 2026, while Audio Overview functionality remains available across Google's notebook/Gemini experiences.

Official references:
- https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-notebook/notebooklm-gemini-notebook/
- https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16047373

Choose when: source grounding matters more than fine-grained voice direction.

## Wondercraft

Best when the creator wants editable scripts, AI hosts/voices, delivery control, collaboration, review, export, and publishing-oriented production.

Official reference:
- https://www.wondercraft.ai/tools/ai-podcast-generator

Choose when: the output is intended to sound like a deliberately produced episode and the user wants more editorial control.

## ElevenLabs GenFM / Studio

Best when the user wants a podcast generated from a document or URL with selectable voices and high-quality speech generation. GenFM can create a podcast script from uploaded material and lets the user review/edit before audio conversion.

Official reference:
- https://elevenlabs.io/docs/help-center/product/distribution-publishing/gen-fm/how-do-i-use-gen-fm

Choose when: voice quality and voice selection are central.

## Privacy decision

For private conversations, first produce a redacted script locally. Uploading raw transcripts to any third-party service should be a deliberate choice based on consent and the service's current privacy terms.
