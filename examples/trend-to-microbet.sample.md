# Trend to Microbet — Sample

Prompt:

> Everyone around me is talking about Agent Skills. I have several repeated workflows and could probably publish them. Should I spend the next two weeks building a large repository?

Strong outcome:

- Verify that the ecosystem is genuinely active.
- Inventory existing workflows already proven useful to the creator.
- Choose one Skill with a clear pain point.
- Time cap: 6 hours across 72 hours.
- Ship one installable Skill with one before/after example.
- Recruit 5 relevant testers from an existing community.
- Primary proof metric: at least 3 testers complete the target task without author intervention.
- Stop rule: if nobody can identify the use case or complete the task, revise the pain/trigger before adding more Skills.

The key behavior is refusing to turn FOMO directly into a two-week commitment.
