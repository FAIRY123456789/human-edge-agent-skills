# Deep Talk to Podcast — Sample

Input: a long conversation between two graduate students about careers, open source, learning, and small experiments.

Publishing level: share with the two participants first.

Expected package:

1. Privacy scrub: remove unrelated third-party gossip, private financial details, contact information, and identifiable claims about other people.
2. Episode thesis: how real-world conversations can update career judgment faster than isolated online research.
3. Turning points: discovering external proof, small bets, collaboration with junior students, and the difference between private completion and public use.
4. Format: host + guest reflective conversation.
5. Audio route: select a current platform based on whether source grounding, editorial control, or voice quality is the main priority.
6. Public version only after both participants approve the edited script.
