# Anti-AI-Wash Checklist

Remove or rewrite when they add no real information:

- slogan-like conclusions;
- repeated “this is important/significant/valuable” without mechanism;
- uniform short paragraphs for dramatic effect;
- generic transitions on every paragraph;
- artificial binary contrast templates;
- abstract nouns replacing concrete actions;
- fake quotes or invented anecdotes;
- emotional intensity stronger than the source;
- a concluding paragraph that merely repeats the introduction.

## Preserve when meaningful

- a slightly unusual but understandable phrase;
- a concrete memory or scene;
- honest hesitation;
- uneven sentence length that reflects thought rhythm;
- first-person judgment when the genre allows it;
- small humor or restraint that belongs to the author.
